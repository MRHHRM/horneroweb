ABOUT

Mauricio Rivero Hernandez

An architect with over 20 years of experience across Norway, Uruguay and international practices. My work spans residential, institutional and large-scale commercial projects — from a BREEAM Outstanding shopping center to nursing homes, private residences and heritage restorations.

I've held roles at AMB Arkitekter, QDP Arkitekter, Pointdesign and N1 Arkitekter in Norway, covering the full project lifecycle from feasibility studies through detailed construction documentation. My practice combines technical precision in Revit and ArchiCAD with hand-drawn concept sketching — and a working knowledge of Norwegian, U.S. and Latin American project standards.
